Closure requirement

Every registered claim identifies its proposition and scope, the upstream SEAM mechanism or executed structural relation that resolves it, retained evidence, the formal result or consequence, any quantitative validation actually present, its downstream comparator where applicable, and the boundary beyond which the claim is not generalized.

A conventional description being downstream is not sufficient by itself; the upstream SEAM cause or resolution must be stated.

Scope discipline

Architecture, methodology, representational, evidence-limited, and application claims are closed only at the scope actually certified. Scope boundaries prevent overstatement and do not represent unfinished foundational construction.
