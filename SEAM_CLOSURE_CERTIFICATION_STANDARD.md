# SEAM Closure Certification Standard — R231

## Required causal order

Every closure must preserve:

```text
first principle -> native derivation -> SEAM-native execution -> frozen result -> downstream comparator/projection
```

A downstream label, equation, category, empirical regularity, or historical operator cannot substitute for upstream resolution.

## Nuclear authority

The historical PNE/EPN `S(X)` spectral operator is not a current nuclear source requirement. A historical certificate may cite it only when describing a sealed historical execution that actually used it.

The locked formation coordinates are `N_low` and `N_high`. `N_most-stable^SEAM` and `N_longest-lived^SEAM` require separately locked native derivations. R229 is a falsification of one candidate interior entropy binding and cannot be cited as a successful selector.

## Required certificate fields

Each current certificate provides: immutable identity/scope; upstream SEAM resolution; native derivation; execution/result linkage; comparator only after native result; quantitative validation where supported; falsifier/decision rule; and exact closure boundary.
