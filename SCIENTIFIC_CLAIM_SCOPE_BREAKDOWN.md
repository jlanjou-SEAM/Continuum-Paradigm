# SEAM Scientific Claim Closure — Scope Breakdown

This report is an aggregation of the **210 individual scientific claim certificates**. It does not change any certificate ruling. Its purpose is to prevent the register total from being read as 210 numerical predictions.

## Closed scientific claims by certified scope kind

- **Quantitative-with-comparator:** **45**
- **Classification/inference scope:** **134**
- **Architecture/mechanism scope:** **31**
- **Total scientific certificates:** **210**

The three reporting kinds are mutually exclusive and sum to 210. They are derived from each certificate's `Quantitative validation` and `Boundary` fields. A certificate is placed in the quantitative group only where its own scope reports a concrete calculated/measured result or an explicit empirical comparator; classification/inference includes certificates whose stated closure is classificatory, ontological, logical, epistemic, inferential, or representational; the remaining mechanism/structural certificates are reported as architecture-level.

## Interpretation

`210/210 scientific claims closed` means that all 210 registered scientific claims have a closed certificate **at their exact registered scope**. It does **not** mean that SEAM has produced 210 independent numerical predictions or 210 independent numerical comparator wins.
